# Threat Model

Covered in v0.1R1:

- artifact mutation, deletion, insertion, and wrong-key verification;
- ledger entry mutation and chain discontinuity;
- model self-validation through inverse crime;
- detectable false-feature injection;
- provenance promotion during rendering; and
- incomplete or malformed capture inputs.

Not covered: key theft, malicious Python runtime, compromised OS, supply-chain attack, trusted timestamping, signer identity, or legal chain-of-custody procedure.
