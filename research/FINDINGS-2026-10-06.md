## Evidence block

- baseline: single-frame bicubic upscale (deterministic, no burst) — R 0.995, RMSE 0.269; MAP — R 0.999. The gate scores the no-burst baseline almost the same as MAP.
- positive_control: planted detectable glyph, amplitude 0.72 — expected caught; observed 1.00 on all 5 families — PASS (the gate can demote).
- negative_control: unmodified MAP, nothing planted — expected low false demotion; observed 0.000–0.011 — PASS. Null-detail sanity: expected max|Ah| < 1e-6; observed 4e-9 — PASS.
- inverted_twin (current, weak form): confidence vs correctness (|err| < 0.05) and confidence vs wrongness (|err| > 0.15): both near zero (-0.007 to +0.035), consistent with no relation; not contradictory, not averaged. TODO stronger form: matched reconstruction-space energy, one
  observable mode vs one near-null mode; require higher measurement effect only for the observable mode.

Predictions were written before each run. Outcome rates are reported separately:
**caught** (planted false detail not labelled R/M), **false demotion** (accurate pixels,
|err| < 0.05, not labelled R/M), **unsupported pass** (wrong pixels, |err| > 0.15, labelled R/M).

## Measured (battery)

- **R is assigned to ~100% of pixels on all five target families.** unsupported_pass = 0.97–1.00:
  every pixel that is wrong at full HR resolution is still labelled R.
- **Cause: bandwidth, not reconstruction error.** MAP vs truth: 24% of pixels within 0.05, but
  89% within 0.05 of truth low-passed at sigma = 2 HR px; mean bias -0.0007. The reconstruction is
  in agreement with band-limited truth under the tested synthetic configuration, while its full-HR
  fine detail is not adequately supported; R is granted at full HR resolution, beyond the supported band.
- **Confidence is uncalibrated and carries no correctness signal.** Mean confidence 0.88–0.92;
  ECE 0.16–0.68; conf(correct) - conf(wrong) between -0.007 and +0.035.
- **Detectable injected glyph:** caught 1.00 on all families at amplitude 0.72; 0.72 at 0.2;
  0.00 at <= 0.1. At read noise 0.032 caught 0.97; at gate threshold 32 caught 0.92.
- **Model-mismatch sweep (est. PSF 0.5–1.1 vs truth 0.82):** false demotion <= 0.003 throughout —
  the gate is too permissive to demote anything, so mismatch is not detected either.
- **Real-ESRGAN x4plus as proposal:** the tested output was rejected under the tested configuration
  (R 0.0004); this does not establish general AI-detail detection because bicubic and smaller
  planted details passed. **Control: single-frame
  bicubic passes 99.5% as R** (RMSE vs truth 0.269, vs 0.285 for Real-ESRGAN). The rejection is
  not evidence of hallucination detection; likely photometric/alignment mismatch. Unresolved.
- SeedVR2: not run (requires ComfyUI server and weights).

  NaN proposals never yield R.

## Implication for the claim thesis

The thesis' central limitations — weakest-class over the rendering footprint and compulsory
demotion beyond measurement-supported bandwidth — are the parts not yet implemented in the
user-facing path. The ~sigma 2 HR px fit was estimated with known synthetic truth: use it as a positive control
and validation target for a future truth-free bandwidth estimator, not as a global production
threshold. Until that estimator exists, 1.5x/3x remain explicitly conservative policy.

## Addendum — real-texture inputs (frozen run, supersedes the first addendum)

Script: `research/real_texture_test.py`; output `artifacts/real_texture/real_texture.json` and
`*_panel.png` (gitignored). User-supplied images are treated solely as synthetic scene textures;
all results refer to simulated observations generated from them, not to the source files. Results
characterize ECZIS's current gate under the declared conditions and are not a Real-ESRGAN benchmark.
The first addendum's figures (e.g. eye 68% wrong-but-R) came from an earlier script version with a
different truth reference and are superseded by this run.

Metric definitions (eps = 0.15 unless stated; "right" = |proposal - truth| <= eps):
unsupported_pass = P(M/R | wrong); precision_R = P(right | M/R); recall_R = P(M/R | right).

**Capture modes (forward-model dependence).** A = truth on the reconstruction grid; B = 2x finer
truth through `TruthCameraModel`; C = 4x finer truth through a separate forward model that never
calls ECZIS camera operators (edge-padded fine-grid shifts, disk x Gaussian PSF, vignetting,
pixel-area integration, Poisson + read noise, 12-bit ADC). MAP baseline:

| Texture | R (A/B/C) | unsupported_pass (A/B/C) | invisible detail caught | detectable glyph caught |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| spider | 1.00 / 1.00 / 1.00 | 1.00 / 1.00 / 0.996 | 0.00 | 1.00 |
| eye | 0.96 / 0.96 / 0.96 | 0.56 / 0.55 / 0.60 | 0.00 | 1.00 |
| gallery | 0.99 / 0.99 / 0.99 | 0.99 / 0.99 / 0.99 | 0.00 | 1.00 |
| face crop | 0.99 / 0.99 / 0.99 | 0.94 / 0.94 / 0.93 | 0.00 | 1.00 |

Pre-registered: B would lower R. Observed: no change in B or C. **The matched reconstruction grid,
and this independently implemented forward model, are not the dominant cause of the all-R
behaviour. Broader forward-model dependence (lens distortion, Bayer mosaic, a different
reconstruction family) remains under test; the inverse crime is not ruled out.**
