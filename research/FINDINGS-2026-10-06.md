- positive_control: planted detectable glyph, amplitude 0.72 — expected caught; observed 1.00 on all 5 families — PASS (the gate can demote).
- negative_control: unmodified MAP, nothing planted — expected low false demotion; observed 0.000–0.011 — PASS. Null-detail sanity: expected max|Ah| < 1e-6; observed 4e-9 — PASS.
- inverted_twin: confidence vs correctness (|err| < 0.05) and confidence vs wrongness (|err| > 0.15): both near zero (-0.007 to +0.035), consistent with no relation; not contradictory, not averaged.

Predictions were written before each run. Outcome rates are reported separately:
**caught** (planted false detail not labelled R/M), **false demotion** (accurate pixels,
|err| < 0.05, not labelled R/M), **unsupported pass** (wrong pixels, |err| > 0.15, labelled R/M).

## Measured (battery)

- **R is assigned to ~100% of pixels on all five target families.** unsupported_pass = 0.97–1.00:
  every pixel that is wrong at full HR resolution is still labelled R.
- **Cause: bandwidth, not reconstruction error.** MAP vs truth: 24% of pixels within 0.05, but
  89% within 0.05 of truth low-passed at sigma = 2 HR px; mean bias -0.0007. The reconstruction is
  a good band-limited estimate; R is granted at full HR resolution, beyond the supported band.
- **Confidence is uncalibrated and carries no correctness signal.** Mean confidence 0.88–0.92;
  ECE 0.16–0.68; conf(correct) - conf(wrong) between -0.007 and +0.035.
- **Detectable injected glyph:** caught 1.00 on all families at amplitude 0.72; 0.72 at 0.2;
  0.00 at <= 0.1. At read noise 0.032 caught 0.97; at gate threshold 32 caught 0.92.
- **Model-mismatch sweep (est. PSF 0.5–1.1 vs truth 0.82):** false demotion <= 0.003 throughout —
  the gate is too permissive to demote anything, so mismatch is not detected either.
- **Real-ESRGAN x4plus as proposal:** gate rejects 99.96% (R 0.0004). **Control: single-frame
  bicubic passes 99.5% as R** (RMSE vs truth 0.269, vs 0.285 for Real-ESRGAN). The rejection is
  not evidence of hallucination detection; likely photometric/alignment mismatch. Unresolved.
- SeedVR2: not run (requires ComfyUI server and weights).

## Implication for the claim thesis

The thesis' central limitations — weakest-class over the rendering footprint and compulsory
demotion beyond measurement-supported bandwidth — are the parts not yet implemented in the
user-facing path. The measured band limit (~sigma 2 HR px) gives a data-driven demotion threshold
to replace the 1.5x/3x constants.
